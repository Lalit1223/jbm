Our Story

Jal Bin Machhali was born from a passion for authentic seafood culinary traditions. Our name, which translates to "Water without Fish," represents our unique approach to creating exquisite seafood dishes that celebrate the essence of the ocean.

Founded in 2020, our journey began with a simple desire: to bring premium seafood experiences to homes across the region. We source the freshest ingredients from sustainable fisheries, ensuring each dish not only delights your palate but also respects the ocean's bounty.

Our commitment goes beyond just serving delicious food. At Jal Bin Machhali, we believe in preserving traditional cooking methods while introducing innovative flavors that tell a story of culture, heritage, and culinary excellence.

Meet the Chef

Yattin Pattil

With over 15 years of culinary expertise, Chef Yattin Pattil is the creative force behind Jal Bin Machhali. His journey began in the coastal regions of South Asia, where he learned traditional seafood recipes passed down through generations.

Chef Yattin combines his deep understanding of flavors with modern culinary techniques, creating dishes that pay homage to tradition while embracing innovation. His passion for sustainable seafood practices influences every aspect of our menu.

"Food is a story that connects us to our heritage, to the environment, and to each other. At Jal Bin Machhali, I strive to tell that story through every dish we create." - Chef Yattin
